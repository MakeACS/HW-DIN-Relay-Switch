# DIN Relay Switch

The DIN relay switch allows for an ACS deployment to be built into a larger electrical cabinet, for more versatile deployments that may require direct electrical interfacing, or pairing with a larger contactor for higher-power applications. 

The Switch integrates its own power supply, so you just need to connect 90-240VAC, or supply 12VDC from an external supply. The HD15 connector is replaced with screw terminals, allowing for easier routing of cabling through wire glands in the form of a pigtail. 

For applications outside of an electrical cabinet, consider the standard [Relay Switch](https://github.com/MakeACS/HW-Relay-Switch).
